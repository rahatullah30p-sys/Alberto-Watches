Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common inquiries regarding servicing intervals, shipping policies, and watch operation.

We recommend a complete maintenance service every 4 to 5 years. This includes disassembling the movement, cleaning all components, replacing worn parts, applying fresh lubricants, replacing water-resistant gaskets, and conducting 500-hour timing tests.

Our warranty covers manufacturing and material defects under normal usage for 5 years from date of purchase. It includes movement failure, dial markers loosening, and hands misalignment. It excludes normal wear-and-tear to leather straps, crystal scratches, or damage caused by unauthorized third-party tampering.

Once you submit a service request, our concierge will issue an insured, prepaid shipping kit complete with a padded travel pouch and tracking documentation. Never send your watch in original presentation boxes, as packaging is not returned post-service.

While our silicon hairsprings protect timepieces up to 4,800 A/m, prolonged exposure to strong magnets (laptops, speakers, MRI machines) can magnetize non-silicon steel balance springs. If your watch suddenly gains time rapidly, visit a boutique for immediate 30-second demagnetization.
