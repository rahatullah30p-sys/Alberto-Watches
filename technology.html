01. Mechanical Assembly
Signature Craft

Micro-Mechanical Hand Assembly

Every mechanical movement contains over 280 individual microscopic components. Our master watchmakers assemble each gear, escapement, spring, and jewel bearing by hand under 10x magnification, ensuring friction-free energy transfer and lifetime accuracy.

02. Hand Openworking
Artistic Finish

Skeletonization & Polishing

By removing non-essential metal from the movement bridges and mainplate, openworking reveals the beating heart of the watch. Every exposed edge is chamfered, angled, and mirror-polished by hand using traditional diamond-paste sticks.

  • ✓ Anglage Finishing: Hand-filed 45-degree beveling on bridge contours.
  • ✓ Côtes de Genève: Parallel decorative wave patterns applied across mainplates.
  • ✓ Perlage Spotting: Circular graining technique applied to internal recessed plates.
Horological Pillars

Core Manufacturing Techniques

Tourbillon Gravity Compensation

Escapement components are enclosed in a lightweight titanium cage that rotates 360° every minute to counteract gravitational rate deviations.

Guilloché Dial Engraving

Intricate geometric guilloché patterns are carved directly onto solid brass dials using century-old, hand-operated rose engine lathes.

Hyper-Baric Sealing

Cases undergo dual dry vacuum and hyper-baric wet pressure chambers, testing resilience up to 300 meters (30 ATM) underwater.

Double-Domed Sapphire Crystal

Synthetic sapphire blocks are diamond-cut and coated with multi-layer anti-reflective treatment for total glare-free legibility.

316L & Rose Gold Metallurgy

Surgical-grade stainless steel paired with 18k electroplated rose gold provides hypoallergenic comfort and scratch resistance.

Swiss Super-LumiNova® Coating

Multiple screen-printed coats of non-radioactive photoluminescent pigment are hand-applied to hands and dial indices for low-light glow.

Technical Mastery

Horological Innovations Explained

Click through our technical breakdowns to explore how precision engineering elevates every Alberto collection.

Traditional metallic balance springs can be magnetized by daily electronics, causing watch speed variance. Our silicon balance springs are completely anti-magnetic, immune to thermal expansion, and drastically improve timekeeping stability.

Bezels are forged from high-tech zirconium oxide powder sintered at 1,500°C. The resulting ceramic is virtually scratch-proof, impervious to UV discoloration, and diamond-polished for a permanent lustrous shine.

Unlike standard cam-actuated timing systems, our column-wheel chronographs provide smooth, instant pusher response without any hand jump or stutter when starting, stopping, or resetting timing functions.
